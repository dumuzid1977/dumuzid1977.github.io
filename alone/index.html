The route

Edinburgh, then north by train

One direct train on Monday 16 August, about 2 hours up the Highland Main Line, and the same train back on Saturday 21 August. In Atholl we walk, take bus 87, or hop on the train to Pitlochry or Kingussie.

By train

One direct train into the Highlands

Edinburgh Waverley to Blair Atholl takes about 2 hours with no change. ScotRail has no peak fares any more: an off-peak return is about £28.60 per adult, and advance singles start around £5.

With Kids for a Quid, a child aged 5–15 travelling with an adult pays £1 return, bought at the ticket office or on the train. That covers our 13-year-old; the 16-year-old pays the adult fare. Airlink 100 bus from the airport: £8.50 adult return, £4.25 child.

For Marián · one day, one roe buck

Roe buck on Straloch

Steven Wade of Woodmill Shootings stalks roe on Straloch and the neighbouring Ashintully Estate, 2,600 ha of hill and woodland north of Pitlochry. His offer (28 Oct 2026): sessions of about 6 hours, morning or afternoon, prices with no VAT. No hunting licence is needed with the stalker's rifle.
