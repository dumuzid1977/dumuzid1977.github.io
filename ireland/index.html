The route

Dublin, then north by bus to the lakes

Bus Éireann route 30 runs from Dublin Busáras via Dublin Airport and Cavan to Enniskillen, 13 times a day, about 3 hours. The same bus takes us straight back to the airport for the flight home.

Two countries, one bus

Enniskillen is in Northern Ireland

There are no border checks on the bus, but Northern Ireland is part of the UK, so each of us needs a UK electronic travel authorisation (ETA): £20 per person, children included, valid for two years. Prices there are in pounds.

Stalking happens in Northern Ireland because a visitor there can shoot an estate's rifle under its stalker's supervision. In the Republic a first-time visitor would need a certified deer course, a Garda firearms certificate and a hunting licence.

For Marián · one morning, one buck

Sika or fallow at Colebrooke Park

Colebrooke Park near Brookeborough, about 7,000 acres plus Favour Royal and Lough Braden, is stalked under Lord Brookeborough's own supervision. Male red, sika and fallow deer are in season in Northern Ireland from 1 August.

What is not possible in August

No pheasant, no Republic stalking

  • Pheasant, Northern Irelandfrom 1 Oct
  • Pheasant, Republicfrom 1 Nov
  • Deer, Republic (visitor)course + licences
  • Clay shooting, Courtlough (Balbriggan)from ~€58, age 14+

Clay shooting needs no licence and the girls can join: a fallback day near Dublin if Colebrooke says no.

The lawIn Northern Ireland anyone 18+ may borrow a rifle on private land in the presence of the occupier or his employee who holds the certificate (Firearms (NI) Order 2004, Sch. 1 para 10).
To confirm firstColebrooke's page asks for proof of rifle ownership or a hunting permit and insurance. Ask whether a first-timer without a licence can go out under that exemption.
Also askThe day rate, a single pickup from Enniskillen instead of the £250-per-week transport, and mid-August conditions.
