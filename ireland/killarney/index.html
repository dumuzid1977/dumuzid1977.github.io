The route

Dublin, then south-west by train

A train every two hours from Dublin Heuston, changing at Mallow, about 3 hours 20 minutes to Killarney. At the end the Flight Link coach runs from Killarney straight to Dublin Airport.

Day by day

Nine days, two chapters

Chapter 1 · Thu 12 – Mon 16 Aug

Dublin, living inside Trinity

We sleep in a Heritage Apartment on Trinity's historic campus, two minutes from the Book of Kells and the Long Room. The Old Library stays open until the end of 2027, before it closes for restoration. Dublin by foot, bus and DART.

12Thu · Aug

Arrive

  • AllRyanair Bratislava → Dublin (about 3 hours). Bus 16 or Dublin Express into town; check in at Trinity from 15:00.
  • AllEvening walk across Front Square, down Grafton Street and over the Ha'penny Bridge.
13Fri · Aug

Trinity day

  • AllTrinity Trails campus tour with students, then the Book of Kells and the Long Room (€33.50 each together).
  • AllAfternoon at the National Museum of Archaeology (free), Merrion Square and St Stephen's Green.
14Sat · Aug

Howth cliffs by DART

  • AllDART train to Howth, the cliff path round the head (seals in the harbour), fish and chips on the pier.
15Sun · Aug

Kilmainham Gaol and the Guinness Storehouse

  • AllGuided tour of Kilmainham Gaol (€8 adult, €4 age 12–17; tickets open 28 days ahead), then the Guinness Storehouse (teenagers get a soft drink).

Chapter 2 · Mon 16 – Fri 20 Aug

Four nights in Killarney

Base: The Grove Cottage, a 19th-century farmhouse on the River Flesk, about 20 minutes' walk from the town. Killarney National Park starts at the edge of town: three lakes, mountains, red deer and a castle.

16Mon · Aug

Train to Killarney

  • AllThe 09:00 from Dublin Heuston, change at Mallow, Killarney 12:21. Walk or a short taxi to the cottage.
  • AllAfternoon walk through the park to Ross Castle on Lough Leane (family ticket €13, guided tour).
17Tue · Aug

The Gap of Dunloe

  • AllBus to Kate Kearney's Cottage, through the Gap by pony and jaunting car (or on foot, 11 km), then the lakes boat from Lord Brandon's Cottage back to Ross Castle.

Jaunting car €140 for a cart of four, cash only.

18Wed · Aug

Bikes to Muckross and Torc

  • AllHire bikes in town, ride the park roads to Muckross House and its Traditional Farms (family €39), on to Torc Waterfall, back along the lake.
19Thu · Aug

Kayaks or the beach

  • AllGuided kayak trip from Ross Castle to Innisfallen Island on Lough Leane, about 2.5 hours.
  • OrLocal Link bus 276 to Inch beach for a surf lesson, on to Dingle for the harbour. Horse riding at Killarney Riding Stables is another option.
20Fri · Aug

Home

  • AllFlight Link 722 coach from Mission Road straight to Dublin Airport, about 4 hours (09:00 or 11:00), fly home.

Or the 07:45 direct train to Heuston and Dublin Express to the airport. Check which days Ryanair flies Dublin → Bratislava.

Killarney · Mon 16 – Fri 20 Aug, 4 nights · still to book

The Grove Cottage, Ballycasheen

19th-century farmhouse on the River Flesk: three bedrooms (double, king, twin), a wood stove, two private gardens. About 20 minutes' walk to town. Rated 4.98 from 633 reviews. About €160–220 a night in August.

See the cottage ↗

Carriage House, on the lake

In the national park on the lakeshore: three en-suite bedrooms, wood stove, big garden. About 25–30 minutes' walk to town; about €250–350 a night.

See the house ↗

In August many Irish cottages let by the week. Ask early whether 4 nights are possible. Closest to town: the Coach House at The Priory, 12–15 minutes' walk.
