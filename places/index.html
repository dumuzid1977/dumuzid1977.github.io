Highland Perthshire · train ~1h50

Pitlochry

A lively Victorian Highland town between Loch Faskally, the River Tummel and Ben Vrackie. Rafting, canyoning and a mountain from the town; roe at Straloch 20 minutes away.

Anstruther, Fife

Red-roofed fishing harbours, golden beaches and the Fife Coastal Path, with St Andrews 40 minutes away. Boat to the Isle of May, surf lessons on West Sands; roe with Woodmill in Fife.

Tweed valley · train ~1h

Melrose, Borders

A red-sandstone abbey town under the three Eildon peaks, on a looping River Tweed. Mountain biking at Glentress, canoeing on the Tweed, castles; the cheapest roe stalking near Kelso.

≈ €3,070 for 5 nights · Harmony Cottage ~£1,250 (≈ €2,230 with a budget flat) · See Harmony Cottage, Melrose ↗
